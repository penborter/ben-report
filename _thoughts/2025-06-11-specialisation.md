This quote from *After Babel* reminds me of the motif in the movie [*Synecdoche, New York*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synecdoche,_New_York) where the wife Adele keeps making smaller and smaller art with greater success, while her husband — the main character — makes art that grows impossibility large and ultimately leads to his demise.
